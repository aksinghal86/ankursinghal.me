    Conducted exposure and risk assessments for PFAS in drinking water, reviewed toxicology and epidemiological evidence, and analyzed the regulatory process for setting drinking water standards. Built a data and analytics hub to organize soil, sediment, groundwater, and surface water results from multiple bellwether cases in the AFFF multidistrict litigation. This work supported litigation leading to major drinking water settlements, including $12.5 billion from 3M and $1.185 billion from DuPont, Chemours, Corteva, and others.

    My team and I also built data systems and software that enabled more than 1,850 public water systems serving about 26 million people to document and pursue claims under the AFFF settlements. These systems supported claims resulting in more than $1.65 billion in recoveries.
