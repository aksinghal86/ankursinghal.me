Data centers are going up fast, and so is everything around them: the data, the news, the science, the regulations, and the concerns from people who live next to them. Air sensors near facilities in Virginia, noise measurements, water use, carbon estimates, new studies, new bills, and local fights over siting. It is a lot to keep track of, and it is scattered across agency websites, preprints, and news stories.

We built this as a resource for everyone to use. It pulls the pieces into one place, organized by topic, so residents, regulators, journalists, lawyers, and scientists can all start from the same information.

## What is in it

- Air. Sensor data near data centers alongside the regulatory monitors for background.
- Noise. Published measurements, with a note on how verifiable each source is.
- Water. What is known about water use and the open questions.
- Carbon. Emissions estimates and where they come from.
