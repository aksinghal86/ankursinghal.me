The national PFAS settlements with water providers required each claimant to document its water sources, sampling results, and production volumes. For a consulting team filing on behalf of many providers at once, the hard part is collecting that information consistently and checking it before the deadline.

## What it does

- An online intake tool where water providers submit their information and documents directly.
- Methods to screen lab reports and production records for eligibility under the settlement terms.
- A data pipeline that tracks every water system and source from first contact to filed claim.
- Dashboards that show the status of the whole effort, and the gaps, at a glance.

Together these supported claims for more than 1,800 water systems across the country.

Access is limited to the client team and participating water providers.
