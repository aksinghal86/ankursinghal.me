Companies entering the California market, or selling through large e-commerce platforms, can face Proposition 65 warning requirements across their entire catalog. Reviewing every product by hand is not realistic when the catalog runs to tens of thousands of items.

## What it does

- Takes a full product inventory, more than 100,000 items in the largest case, and screens it against the Proposition 65 list.
- Uses product categories, materials, and known chemical associations to flag the items most likely to need attention.
- Narrows a huge list down to the short one that deserves an exposure assessment.

The screening rests on more than ten years of Proposition 65 exposure assessments for the most litigated listed chemicals.

This tool is used for client work and is not publicly available.
